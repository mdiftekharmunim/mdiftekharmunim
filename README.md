## About Me

I'm an undergraduate student pursuing a **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering at Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB)**.

I'm passionate about **web development** and enjoy turning ideas into working products, from clean interfaces to the logic behind them. Beyond the web, I want to understand computers more deeply, which is why I'm currently exploring the theory side of computer science, including **automata and the theory of computation**.

I learn best by building, so most of what you'll find here is practice turned into real projects.
